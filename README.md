# Linus & Wesley Jungle Adventure

A side-scrolling dinosaur platformer for the whole family: pick Daddy, Mommy, Kyla, Linus or Wesley. Play it by opening `index.html` in a browser. It works on a phone held sideways (touch buttons), a keyboard or a game controller.

- **Single Player:** choose any of the 5 heroes.
- **Two Player Co-op:** Player 1 and Player 2 each pick a different hero and play together on the same screen.
- **Level Select:** replay any level you've unlocked.
- **Collections:** dinosaur eggs and achievements you've found.

Three jungle levels (Jungle Trail, Vine River, Ancient Ruins) with coins, spicy noodles for fire breath, Mini Carnotaurus to stomp, 28 hidden dinosaur eggs, and a Giant Carnotaurus boss guarding the Tree House Castle.

## Controls
| | Move | Jump | Fire |
|---|---|---|---|
| Single player | ← → or A D | Space / ↑ / W | X / F / Enter |
| Player 1 (co-op) | A D | W | F |
| Player 2 (co-op) | ← → | ↑ | Enter |

Gamepads: stick/D-pad to move, A to jump, B/X to fire. Press P or Esc to pause.
